Our identity

Values that guide every move

At Young Samurai, martial technique is not an end in itself. It serves a deeper human development, guided by five lived values — not just displayed.

01
Discipline
Commit consistently to training, service and collective life.
02
Service
Give your time because it's right, not for recognition.
03
Respect
Treat every person with kindness — fellow practitioner, beneficiary, partner.
04
Solidarity
Support your group and reach out to those in need.
05
Transmission
Learn, grow, and pass it on — at every training session, at every camp.
Season 2026–27

A structured program that builds leaders

Two groups working together. The older ones mentor the younger ones — that is their responsibility, and that is what truly shapes them. Community service is a prerequisite for grade advancement.

Fondation Group
Little Samurai
6–9 ans
Learning, discovering, being guided by the older students under the supervision of a professional instructor. Participates in all events. Building the foundations of discipline and confidence.
Transmission Group
Young Samurai
10–15 ans
Each older student is responsible for 2–3 younger ones during events. This responsibility is a prerequisite for grade advancement. The 13–15 year olds become assistant instructors.
Professional coaching
Led by a certified martial arts instructor
Little Samurai and Young Samurai are coached by a certified instructor with years of experience — not only by volunteers. The older students assist and pass on knowledge under direct adult supervision. It is this combination that makes the program exceptional: technical expertise and human transmission.

Our commitment

Serving means growing

Community service is not just one activity among others. It is a prerequisite for grade advancement. Our young people serve real people, in their neighborhood, throughout the season.

🤝
Young people in difficulty
Sports activities with vulnerable young people. Physical activity as a universal language.
🛍️
Food & clothing aid
Supporting local solidarity organizations — handling, welcoming, collections.
👴
Intergenerational connection
Visits and activities with the elderly. Demonstrations, games, human presence.
🌿
Nature & Environment
Nature work during Ardennes camps. Managing nature reserves and maintaining trails.
